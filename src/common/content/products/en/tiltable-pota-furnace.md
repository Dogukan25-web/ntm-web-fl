NTM tiltable crucible furnaces are designed to achieve maximum efficiency and metal quality in melting processes. Preventing direct contact between the flame and the molten metal inside the crucible minimizes oxidation and combustion losses, ensuring purer and more controlled production.

The crucible, housed within a body structure featuring high insulation properties, is heated evenly and homogeneously through advanced combustion systems. Thanks to the hydraulically driven tilting mechanism, molten metal can be transferred to the casting line quickly, safely, and precisely. This structure enhances operator safety while supporting process continuity.

High-performance refractory linings used in the furnace body minimize heat losses and contribute to long service life with low maintenance requirements. With their durable construction and optimized thermal management, tiltable crucible furnaces offer significant advantages in energy savings and sustainable production.
