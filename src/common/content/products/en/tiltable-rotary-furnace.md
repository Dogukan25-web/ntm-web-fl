Developed by NTM, tiltable rotary furnace systems are manufactured to be fully compatible with different fuel technologies through a combustion unit integrated into a hydraulically controlled lid structure. Depending on plant requirements, the system can be configured with natural gas, oxy-fuel, or fuel-oil-based burner options, delivering high efficiency and stable combustion performance.

Thanks to its central control infrastructure and specially developed automation software, all operations can be managed through a single control panel. With a remote control system that provides maximum flexibility during processing, furnace functions can be controlled quickly, safely, and without interruption.

Precision load measurement sensors integrated into the furnace body monitor the charged material quantity in real time, enabling numerical tracking of the production process. This allows businesses to perform efficiency analyses based on accurate data and sustainably optimize production performance.
