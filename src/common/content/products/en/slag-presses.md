NTM slag press systems are designed to increase recovery rates by enabling the controlled processing of hot slag generated during the melting process. The slag is placed into specially designed press trays and compressed under high pressure, allowing maximum recovery of the valuable metal it contains.

During the pressing process, the temperature of the slag is reduced in a controlled manner, while a safer structure is obtained for subsequent processing and disposal stages. This enhances workplace safety and minimizes environmental risks.

Customizable according to different production capacities and slag densities, the systems deliver stable performance even under long-term and intensive operating conditions thanks to their powerful hydraulic infrastructure and durable structural design. Slag presses represent both an economical and sustainable recovery solution for industrial facilities.
