NTM DE-OX casting machines provide high-speed and process-safe production of deoxidizer ingots by precisely transferring molten metal to molds through a continuous conveying line. Thanks to integrated air and water cooling systems, the metal is solidified in a controlled manner inside the molds, ensuring stable product quality.

Equipped with a fully automated control infrastructure, the systems guarantee repeatability and quality standards at every stage of the production process. The continuous-operation line structure offers operational advantages through uninterrupted production performance and reduced downtime.

In addition to standard mold options suitable for different production capacities, the systems offer a flexible structure that can be integrated into facilities of any scale, thanks to custom-developed molds and line solutions tailored to specific plant requirements.
