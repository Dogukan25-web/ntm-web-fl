NTM liquid metal mixer systems ensure homogeneous chemical composition and temperature distribution by creating continuous and balanced mixing within the molten metal bath. This prevents quality fluctuations during production and helps maintain final product standards.

Thanks to their high-temperature-resistant mechanical structure and optimized mixing geometry, the mixers create effective circulation in the metal bath, reducing the risk of segregation. At the same time, they contribute to the balanced distribution of alloying elements, making melting and refining processes more controlled.

Integrated with automation systems, liquid metal mixers enable precise management of process parameters while supporting optimized energy consumption and improved furnace efficiency. With their durable design, they deliver reliable performance under long-term and intensive production conditions.
