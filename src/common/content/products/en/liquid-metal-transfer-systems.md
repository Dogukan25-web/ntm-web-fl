NTM liquid metal transfer channel systems ensure production continuity by enabling the controlled routing of molten metal from furnaces to casting lines and intermediate process points. Thanks to optimized flow geometry, metal transfer remains stable, while splashing and flow losses are minimized.

Custom-sized according to plant capacity and production pace, the transfer channel systems can be seamlessly integrated into various line configurations. Manufactured using high-temperature-resistant special alloys and refractory linings, the structure offers long service life under heavy industrial conditions.

Designed to operate in coordination with automation and feeding systems, the transfer channels enable precise management of flow control. As a result, process safety is enhanced, while casting quality and operational efficiency are sustainably improved.
