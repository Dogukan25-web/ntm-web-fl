NTM ingot casting systems create a high-speed and stable production process by ensuring the continuous and controlled transfer of molten metal into molds after melting. Thanks to the conveyor-based line structure, casting operations proceed uninterrupted, while integrated air and water cooling systems enable the metal to solidify into the desired form in a short time.

Standard and custom-designed molds, available in various sizes and geometries, fully meet customer-specific production requirements. Equipped with a robust mechanical infrastructure, optimized cooling technologies, and a PLC-supported automation system, the lines can operate at high capacity with minimal operator intervention.

All process parameters managed through the central control system ensure consistent production quality, while the long-lasting structural design allows the systems to deliver reliable performance even under heavy industrial conditions.
