NTM copper melting systems are developed to achieve maximum efficiency and low operating costs in the recovery of scrap copper and metallurgical residues. The specially designed furnace geometry creates intensive metal circulation throughout the melting process, shortening processing times and maximizing recovery rates.

Thanks to advanced combustion and control infrastructure, oxygen and natural gas ratios can be precisely adjusted according to process requirements. This structure optimizes energy consumption while enabling high melting performance and low emission levels. At the same time, it provides a production process compliant with environmental sustainability standards.

Special gas injection systems integrated into the furnace body ensure controlled delivery of required gases into the molten metal during the refining stage. This facilitates the removal of unwanted elements, improving metal purity and overall product quality.

Equipped with high-temperature-resistant refractory linings and efficiency-focused burner technologies, the systems deliver long service life and stable performance even under heavy operating conditions, providing businesses with a reliable production infrastructure.
