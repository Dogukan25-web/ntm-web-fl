NTM T-Ingot (SOW) casting lines enable the production of ingots with standardized weights and homogeneous structure by precisely transferring molten metal into molds through automated systems. Operating on a fully automated principle, the line structure minimizes human intervention during production, delivering high operational efficiency.

Before production, molds are automatically prepared using integrated surface coating and preheating units. This increases casting quality while extending mold service life. Once solidification is complete, the ingots are safely released from the molds by the system and directed to stacking and shipping lines.

Thanks to advanced automation infrastructure and durable mechanical construction, T-Ingot casting lines provide stable performance and long-term operational reliability under continuous production conditions. With low labor requirements, high production capacity, and optimized process management, they offer businesses a sustainable competitive advantage.
