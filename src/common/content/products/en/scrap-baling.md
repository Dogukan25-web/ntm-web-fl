---

NTM scrap baling systems compress scattered and bulky scrap materials under high pressure into regular and transportable bales. This structure contributes to more efficient use of storage space and more controlled logistics operations.

Press systems that can be customized according to different scrap types and plant requirements are designed to produce bales in the desired dimensions. As a result, feeding into the production line becomes more planned and organized, reducing operational complexity.

Thanks to their durable body structure and powerful hydraulic infrastructure, scrap baling presses deliver long-lasting and reliable performance under intensive working conditions. By ensuring effective scrap management, these systems increase production efficiency while minimizing labor losses and transportation costs.
