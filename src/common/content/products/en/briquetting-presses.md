NTM briquetting press systems provide maximum efficiency in recycling processes by compressing ferrous and non-ferrous scrap materials into compact and homogeneous briquettes under high pressure. Reduced in volume and standardized in form, these materials offer significant operational advantages in both logistics and melting stages.

Equipped with a robust hydraulic infrastructure and durable mechanical construction, the systems can be configured to adapt to different types of scrap. This enables irregularly shaped waste materials to be transformed into melt-ready and easily processable raw materials.

Thanks to optimized pressing technology, storage space requirements are reduced, transportation costs are lowered, and furnace feeding processes become more controlled. Briquetting presses represent a strategic investment for facilities aiming for sustainable production and efficient resource utilization.
