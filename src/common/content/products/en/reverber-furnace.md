Developed by NTM, reverberatory furnaces are specially designed according to different production capacities and process requirements, delivering high performance in melting and holding applications. Depending on plant conditions and customer expectations, these furnaces can be configured with regenerative, recuperative, or cold-air burner systems, prioritizing energy efficiency and combustion stability.

Manufactured with fixed or tilting body options, the systems feature a wide front door design that enables fast and safe charging, slag removal, and maintenance operations. Optionally integrated magnetic stirring systems ensure homogeneous circulation of molten metal, reducing melting time and improving product quality.

Supported by optional equipment such as automatic charging and slag discharge cars, reverberatory furnaces help reduce operating costs while elevating workplace safety standards. All furnace functions and operational data can be monitored via a central control panel, enabling uninterrupted and fully controlled process management.
