NTM liquid metal pump systems provide maximum stability in production processes by ensuring the controlled and safe transfer of molten metal between furnaces, ladles, and casting lines. At the same time, they contribute to maintaining a homogeneous metal bath by creating continuous circulation inside the furnace.

Thanks to their optimized hydrodynamic design and high-temperature-resistant structure, the systems reduce energy consumption while minimizing metal losses. This enables balanced heat distribution and more precise control of process parameters.

With their modular structure that can be integrated into production lines and an automation-supported control system, liquid metal pumps offer significant advantages to businesses in improving furnace efficiency and ensuring sustainable production performance.
