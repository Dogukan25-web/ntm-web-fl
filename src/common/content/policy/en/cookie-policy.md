# Cookie Policy

As NURİMAK TEKNİK MAKİNA SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ (“Şirket”), cookies are used on our website to improve user experience and ensure the secure delivery of our services.

This document has been prepared to explain the purposes for which cookies are used.

---

## 1. What Is a Cookie?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device through your browser by the websites you visit.

These files help the website operate more efficiently.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies Used

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the basic functioning of the website.

Without these cookies:

- Forms may not function properly
- Security mechanisms may be disabled
- Session operations may not be carried out reliably

### Analytical and Performance Cookies

These cookies are used to analyze how the website is used.

Analytical tags operating through Google Tag Manager fall within this scope.

Such cookies are activated only with the user’s explicit consent.

---

## 3. Purposes of Using Cookies

Cookies may be used for the following purposes:

- Ensuring website security
- Monitoring technical performance
- Improving user experience
- Enhancing service quality

---

## 4. Managing Cookies

Users may change their cookie preferences at any time.

To do so, they may:

- Use their browser settings
- Use the “Manage Cookie Preferences” link on the website

Disabling cookies completely may cause some website functions to not operate properly.

---

## 5. Third-Party Cookies

Cookies placed by third-party service providers may be used on the website.

These services are subject to their own privacy policies.

---

## 6. Updates

This cookie policy may be updated when necessary. The current version is published on the website and becomes effective as of its publication date.
