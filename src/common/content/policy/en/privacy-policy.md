# Privacy Policy

NURİMAK TEKNİK MAKİNA SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ (“Şirket”) undertakes to protect the privacy of users visiting its website and the security of their personal data.

This policy explains the scope under which data collected through the website are processed and protected.

---

## 1. Information Collected

The following information may be collected through our website:

- Full Name
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Company Name
- Message and Project Details
- IP address and technical usage data

These data are obtained through users voluntarily filling out website forms.

---

## 2. Purposes of Using the Information

The collected information is used for the following purposes:

- Responding to users
- Preparing and delivering quotations
- Managing service processes
- Managing business development and operational processes
- Fulfilling legal obligations

---

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Essential cookies may be used on our website to ensure its core functionality.

Non-essential cookies (analytical, advertising, or marketing cookies) are not activated without the user’s explicit consent.

Google Tag Manager infrastructure is used. Analytical or marketing tags are activated only if the user selects the “Accept All” option.

You may change your cookie preferences at any time.

For more detailed information, you may review the **Cookie Policy** page.

---

## 4. Information Security

Personal data:

- Are protected against unauthorized access
- Are stored on secure server infrastructures
- Are accessible only to authorized personnel

The Company takes technical and administrative measures to ensure data security.

---

## 5. Data Retention Period

Personal data are retained for the period required by the purpose of processing and are deleted, destroyed, or anonymized in accordance with applicable legislation at the end of this period.

---

## 6. Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites. Our Company is not responsible for the data processing policies of such websites.

---

## 7. Updates

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. The current version is published on the website and becomes effective as of its publication date.
